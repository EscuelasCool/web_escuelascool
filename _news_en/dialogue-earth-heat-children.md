During the **Global Heat Forum 2026** in Bangkok, the environmental media outlet **Dialogue Earth** interviewed **Yasna Palmeiro-Silva**, Principal Investigator of EscuelasCool, and **Tolullah Oni**, founder and CEO of UrbanBetter. The video, *Heat is shaping children's futures*, was published on 23 November 2026.

The conversation explores why extreme heat does not affect everyone equally and why children and adolescents are more vulnerable than adults. It also highlights a gap that lies at the heart of EscuelasCool: the long-term effects of exposure to extreme heat during childhood are still poorly understood. Finally, it considers the role young people can play in the solutions.
