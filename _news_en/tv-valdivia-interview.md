On 26 October 2026, **Tamara Hoffmann Ferrada**, who leads the qualitative component of EscuelasCool, was interviewed on **TV Valdivia** about the effects of El Niño 2026–27 and climate change on schools in Latin America, a few days before our webinar *El Niño and the children*.

In the conversation, Tamara explains that the project grew out of seeing the harm extreme heat is already causing in the region, and asking what this means for children who learn in classrooms with no protection from the heat. She describes why it is a silent threat, with effects on attention, learning and wellbeing, including eco-anxiety, her own field of research.

She also explains how we will work in Chile, Colombia and Peru: measuring temperature in classrooms and running short attention tests on tablets, alongside conversations with students, teachers and school leaders about their experience. The ultimate goal is to produce public policy guidelines for more resilient schools, grounded in the evidence and in what school communities themselves tell us.
